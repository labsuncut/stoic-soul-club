Help

Draft policies for the trial store. Stoic must confirm every fee, time and rule before real customers order.

Delivery

We deliver across Jordan. Fees by area are shown at checkout. Amman orders usually arrive in 1 to 3 working days.

Exchanges and returns

Wrong size? Message us within 7 days of delivery and we'll swap it, as long as the piece is unworn with its tags on.

Your order

After checkout you get a private order page. Bookmark it to follow your order from received to delivered.

Privacy

We collect your name, phone, email (if you give it) and address only to deliver your order and contact you about it. We do not sell your details or use advertising trackers. Card details are handled by the payment provider, never stored by us. To see or delete your details, message us.

Terms

Prices are in Jordanian dinars. An order is confirmed when you receive your order page. If an item turns out to be unavailable, we contact you and refund anything paid for it.
