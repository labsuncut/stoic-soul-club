Help

Fastest answer: message us

Sizes, delivery, an order: send us a message and one of us replies.

WhatsApp us Instagram DM

Delivery

Free inside Amman: one of us brings it to you. Anywhere else in Jordan, 3 JOD by courier. We message you on WhatsApp to arrange the time.

Payment

Pay cash when your order arrives, or by CliQ. After you order, your order page shows the CliQ alias and the number to write in the note.

Size swaps and defects

Wrong size? Message us within 7 days of delivery and we'll swap it, as long as it's unworn with the tags on. The swap delivery is on you. If something arrives with a defect, we replace it for free.

Your order

After checkout you get a private order page. Bookmark it to follow your order from received to delivered.

Privacy

We collect your name, phone, email (if you give it) and address only to deliver your order and contact you about it. We don't sell your details and we don't use advertising trackers. To see or delete your details, message us.

Terms

Prices are in Jordanian dinars. If a piece turns out to be unavailable after you order, we contact you and refund anything you paid for it.
