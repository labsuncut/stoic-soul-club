# Editing the site (for Sanad)

Everything on the site is changed from the dashboard at `/staff/`. No code, no developer. Log in with the staff password; it is in the founders' password manager.

Changes show on the site within about 20 seconds. If a page looks old, pull to refresh.

## Products and stock
- **Stock** is the number of pieces on the shelf, per size and colour. Change the number in the box; it saves as you type. 0 means sold out for that size.
- **Add a size** with the row under the list (size, colour, stock).
- **Edit** opens the product's details: name, price in JOD, chapter (kaizen, tranquility, vengeance or a new one), category, description, fit, materials, care, story, photos, status.
- **Status**: *Draft* (hidden), *Available* (on sale), *Sold out* (shown without an order button), *Archived* (only in the archive, not for sale). "Available" with every size at 0 shows as sold out by itself.
- **Lead piece** puts the product in the big frame at the top of the drop.
- Keep it true: only write materials, weights and measurements you have checked. Leave a field empty rather than guessing; empty fields are simply not shown.

## Photos
- **Upload** takes a JPG, PNG or WebP from your phone or computer. The browser shrinks it to 1600 px before sending, so a straight-from-camera photo is fine.
- The first photo is the main one; the second shows on hover on the shop grid. The caption box under each photo is optional and shows under the photo on the product page (for example "Back print, on body"). Portrait photos work best on product frames; the cover is landscape on desktop.
- **Paste a link** works for a photo that already lives on the internet (an https address).
- Logo: a PNG with a transparent background. Until one is set the butterfly mark is used.
- The tee front and back views on the KAIZEN, TRANQUILITY and VENGEANCE tee pages are cut from the brand's own 2024 design sheet; replace them with real photos of the tees (front, back, on body) when you have them.

## Chapters
A chapter is a collection: KAIZEN, TRANQUILITY, VENGEANCE and the next ones. Each has a number, a one-line meaning, a description, an accent colour, photos and a status (*Draft*, *Current*, *Archived*). Current chapters appear on the home page; archived ones stay in the archive.

## Lookbooks
One lookbook per photoshoot or campaign: title, chapter, photographer credit, a short intro, then the photos in order with a caption and an alt text (a one-line description for people who cannot see the photo). Tag a photo *Community* when a customer took it (only with their permission); those show under "Seen in the wild" instead of the film strip. The featured lookbook feeds the film strip on the home page. Only *Published* lookbooks are visible.

## Site tab
- **WhatsApp number**: international format, digits only (9627XXXXXXXX). Empty turns ordering off: the button reads "Ordering opens soon". Change it only to a number someone answers.
- **Instagram handle** without the @.
- **Announcement bar**: for things that are decided (a drop date, a closure). Empty means no bar. No countdowns or "only 3 left".
- **Home page**: headline, button text, cover photo or a short muted video, caption, the philosophy lines.
- **Story page**: the brand's story in the founders' words, one line per paragraph.
- **Policies and wording**: how ordering works, delivery, payment, swaps. These show on product pages, the footer and the contact page. Write only what Stoic actually does.
- **Switches**: restock questions on sold-out pieces (only if someone will answer), anonymous counts (page views and WhatsApp clicks; no cookies, nothing personal), and **Site is public** (lets Google list the site; leave off until the founders approve the launch).

## Inquiries tab
Warnings at the top tell you when ordering is off (no WhatsApp number saved), when photo uploads are not connected, and that the site is still hidden from Google. Below them: counts of page views and WhatsApp clicks today, this week and this month, the most viewed pieces, where visitors came from (Instagram or direct), and sizes that are at 0. A WhatsApp click is an inquiry, not a sale: the sale happens in the chat.

## If something breaks
- "Photo uploads are not set up yet": the R2 bucket is not connected; see LAUNCH.md step 5. Paste a link meanwhile.
- "Wrong password" eight times in a row locks login for 15 minutes.
- Anything else: send the founders a screenshot and what you clicked.
